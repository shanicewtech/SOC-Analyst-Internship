## Overview

This repository documents my completion of Week 1, Task 1 of the SOC Analyst Internship with TechBiz Academy.

The task focused on developing an understanding of cybersecurity fundamentals, the responsibilities of a Security Operations Centre (SOC), and the importance of identifying and responding to potential security threats.

## Topics Covered

- Introduction to Cybersecurity
- The CIA Triad: Confidentiality, Integrity and Availability
- Threats, Vulnerabilities and Risks
- Security Operations Centre (SOC)
- SOC Analyst Roles: Level 1, Level 2 and Level 3
- Incident Responders and Threat Hunters
- Blue Team vs Red Team
